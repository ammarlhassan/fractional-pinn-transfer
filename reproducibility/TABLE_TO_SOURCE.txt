TABLE-TO-SOURCE MAPPING
========================
Maps every paper table (main.tex) to its JSON data source.
All JSONs are in reproducibility/data/.

NOTE: The composite NN comparison (tab:composite) was REMOVED from the paper.
      The rerun script used a broken dummy model (naive 2-scalar weighted average
      instead of the real Meng-Karniadakis architecture). The composite_alpha0.5.json
      in results/rerun/ contains INVALID data and is excluded from this package.
